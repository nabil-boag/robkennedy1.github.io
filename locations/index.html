The Map

Locations

Places the party has been — and places they have only heard whispered of.

Visited

Velkynvelve

A drow slaving outpost in the Underdark, perched above a yawning cavern and ringed by giant spiders’ webs. The party began their tale here as prisoners in its slave pen, held until a drow party from Menzoberranzan could march them off to be sold. It is commanded by the cruel priestess Ilvara.

The outpost sits high against the cavern wall, its rooms carved into hanging stalactites and hollowed out of the rock, all joined by steps and swaying rope bridges. A waterfall falls through the middle of it into a pool far below, and a thick blanket of spider silk is strung beneath the walkways. The three passages out of the cavern — north towards Menzoberranzan, west towards the Darklake, south — all open at ground level, watched from guarded alcoves above.

The party escaped it by cutting down through the webs and swimming the pool. They left it burning behind them: as they fled, demons poured out of the north passage and fell upon the outpost, smashing the bridges and towers while the drow loosed crossbows into the air.

Sloobludop

A kuo-toa town on the eastern shore of the Darklake, and the home of Shuushar the Awakened — seven days west of Velkynvelve through tunnels, fungus swamp and spiderweb.

From a distance it looked like a huge tangle of reeds rising into the darkness, lit by patches of phosphorescence; close up, a stack of rickety towers lashed together by rope and plank walkways in haphazard patterns, built as though its inhabitants expected to swim between the levels.

The party never got to trade there, or rest there, or put in the good word they owed Grimnar. They arrived as prisoners, were claimed as offerings, and were paraded into a ceremony that ended with the archpriest of the Sea Mother murdering his own daughter on the altar.

Her blood called Demogorgon out of the lake. The party’s last sight of Sloobludop was its towers being swept into the water a row at a time.

The Darklake

Not one lake but a chain of them — a vast series of connected caverns, black water and stone columns running from the surface of the water all the way up into the dark, further than any light the party had could reach.

The west passage out of Velkynvelve leads here, and it was Joy, newly thrown into the slave pen, who first passed on what she had overheard about which way to go. Sloobludop stands four hours east along the shore; Gracklstugh lies somewhere on the opposite side.

It is said to be the way to find transport and cross the Underdark. It is also where Demogorgon was waiting.

The Silken Paths

A gigantic network of spiderwebs strung across a chasm on the road to the Darklake, criss-crossing from wall to wall and weaving in and out of tunnels — the only reasonable way west, and, as Shuushar cheerfully confirmed, not remotely safe.

Anything without a climbing speed crosses at half pace on hands and knees. The webs are not especially sticky, but the drop is very long, and anything that takes a hit up there risks going over the edge.

The party crossed it guided by the goblins Yuck Yuck and Spider Bait, cut the halfling Fergus Rumblefoot out of a cocoon along the way, and lost Jimjar to a spectator before they reached the far side.

Heard Of

Menzoberranzan

A drow city. A party from Menzoberranzan was expected at Velkynvelve to take the prisoners away to be sold as slaves — but it was running days late.

Neverlight Grove

The home of the myconid Stool and his people. Stool has asked the party to escort him there, promising they will be given sanctuary among his kind.

Gauntlgrym

The home of Eldeth Feldrun’s family. Before her murder, she asked Ilyrion to return her warhammer and shield to her kin here, should she not survive.

Blingdenstone

A deep gnome city in the Underdark, from which the drow drove the gnomes out. The deep gnome spy Jimjar was sent from the surface to investigate its current status.

Gracklstugh

A duergar city on the far side of the Darklake, and Buppido’s home — he means to travel with the party a while yet, then make his own way back to it.

By his account it is enormous and dangerous and he is very fond of it. It is less known for fair maidens than for its choking smog, thrown up by the many furnaces that burn there, and it is home to a red dragon that keeps those furnaces lit. Thibaud offered to slay it. Buppido pointed out that this would be killing the local industry.

The Lost Tomb of Kaine

A tomb somewhere in the Underdark, said by the halfling Fergus Rumblefoot to hold treasure. He was hunting it with three companions when gnolls killed them and spiders took him.

He says he can still find the way. Asked what guards it, he answered: spiders. Neither Shuushar nor anyone else the party asked had ever heard of Kaine.
