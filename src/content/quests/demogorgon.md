When **Ploopploopeen** killed his daughter on the altar at Sloobludop, her blood called something up out of the **Darklake**.

Thirty feet and more of it stood out of the water: a single torso carrying **two heads like enormous, furious baboons**, oily tentacles thrashing, its eyes and throat burning red with what could only be described as bloodlust and madness. Every kuo-toa in the town — both sides of the civil war — dropped what they were doing and clutched their heads. **Rhys** looked at it too long and had to be dragged away babbling.

**Jebediah** recognised it from his religious training: **Demogorgon**, Prince of Demons, one of the most powerful beings in the multiverse.

It flattened Sloobludop while the party ran. This is what the dreams were warning about, and what the spectator meant when it said *demons are rising in the underworld*.
