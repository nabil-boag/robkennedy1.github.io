As the party dropped into the pool beneath Velkynvelve, every one of them heard the same words spoken directly into their minds:

> *Flesh for the faceless lord.*

The **grey ooze** that rose out of the water was slow, mindless-seeming and easily outrun — and yet something had spoken. Nobody knows what a faceless lord is, or whether the ooze was its servant, its mouthpiece, or merely its leavings.

It is the second time the deep has spoken to them. See also **Grim Portents** — the dreams of tentacles, buzzing and madness that troubled Ilyrion, Rhys and Jebediah on their last night in the pen.
