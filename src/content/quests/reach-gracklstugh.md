With Sloobludop destroyed and Demogorgon still standing in the ruins of it, the only destination left is the duergar city of **Gracklstugh** — three or four weeks east along the shore of the Darklake.

**Buppido** and **Hemeth** are both going home there, **Sarith** has become strangely eager to see it, **Fergus** reckons the **Lost Tomb of Kaine** lies about halfway along the route, and **Neverlight Grove** turns out to be north of the city, which settles Stool's journey too.

Everyone wants to go to the same place for once. It only took a god destroying the alternative.
