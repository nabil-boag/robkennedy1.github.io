Return **Eldeth's** warhammer and shield to her family in **Gauntlgrym**, as she asked of Ilyrion before she was murdered.

The promise stands; the means of keeping it does not. Rather than risk a room full of drow for a pile of gear, the party abandoned the armoury raid on their way out — so Eldeth's warhammer and shield are still sitting in **Velkynvelve**.
