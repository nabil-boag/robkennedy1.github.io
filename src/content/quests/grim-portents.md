On their final night in the pen, three of the party were visited by disturbing dreams. **Rhys** wandered lost through an endless maze of tunnels as dark shadows rose and reached for him. **Ilyrion** walked a hellscape where oily tentacles brushed against him and a great buzzing and howling rose from some huge, awful creature. **Jebediah** saw people clutching their heads and screaming in madness, their wounds bursting with maggots and insects. Rhys and Ilyrion woke at the very same instant. All three are left with the dread certainty that something **far worse than the drow** lurks in the depths of the Underdark.

*What could the dreams mean?*
