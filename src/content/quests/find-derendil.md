**Derendil** did not come out of Sloobludop. Nobody saw him fall, nobody saw him run, and there was no body in the part of the ruins the party got close enough to search before Demogorgon drove them off.

He may be dead under the town. He may have bolted in the dark and be walking the shore somewhere. If he was telling the truth about being **Prince Derendil of Nelrindenvane**, there is a kingdom in the High Forest that will never know what became of him either.
