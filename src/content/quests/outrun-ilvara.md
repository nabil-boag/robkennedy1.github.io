title: "Outrun Ilvara"
kind: quest

No prisoners have ever broken out of Velkynvelve before, and **Ilvara** has taken it personally. She has sent an advance party of soldiers after the escapees and ridden out behind them with her commanders.

She does not know which of the three passages the party took. Keeping it that way is the whole game: the duergar trader **Grimnar** has been persuaded to point the drow down the wrong path, and everything the party does on the road — how fast they travel, what they leave behind them, who they talk to — will decide whether the pursuit closes or falls away.
