kind: quest

The duergar trader **Grimnar** agreed to lie to the drow about which way the party went. His price: that when they reached **Sloobludop**, **Shuushar** would put in a good word for his company with the kuo-toa and open up the trade there.

Shuushar is dead, Sloobludop has been destroyed, and there is no trade left there to open. The party held up their end of nothing at all, through no fault of their own.

They did get to tell him, at least. Grimnar turned up again past Dhurgrimm, having kept his side of the bargain perfectly, and took the news that his new market had been flattened by Demogorgon remarkably well. He'll find new trade routes.
