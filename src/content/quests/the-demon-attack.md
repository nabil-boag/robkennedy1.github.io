The party's escape was covered by an attack nobody expected. As they reached the cavern floor, alarm horns went up across Velkynvelve — not for the escaped prisoners, but for what was coming out of the **north passage**.

Two enormous **decaying, vulture-like demons** flew in pursued by four bloated, droning things like giant mosquitoes. They fought each other in the air above the outpost, smashing through rope bridges and slamming into the drow towers, while the garrison shouted and loosed crossbow bolts at all six.

Nothing about them looked natural — not even in the way that Underdark monsters look unnatural. One of the vulture-demons crashed wounded across the western exit and had to be killed before the party could get out.

The dreams warned of something worse than the drow. Something worse than the drow has now been seen.
