An Underdark god of murder. Jebediah recognised the posed body of the murdered **Eldeth Feldrun** as an offering made to Diinkarazan — suggesting a worshipper of this dark god moved among the prisoners or the drow.

One did. **Buppido** confessed in Dhurgrimm that he strangled Eldeth as a sacrifice to *his god*, believing it bought the prisoners their escape — and the next morning, they did escape. He died without naming his god, but the offering Jebediah recognised was his.
