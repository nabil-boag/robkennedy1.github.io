**Fergus Rumblefoot** wants to go back for the treasure that killed his last party, and he wants this one to come with him.

The party went. Past the empty city of Dhurgrimm, in a forest of mushrooms, every one of them heard the same soft woman's voice in their heads: *I have been trapped in the dark for so long. Won't you help to free me?* They suddenly knew which way the entrance lay, and found it — a bronze-sheathed door in a marble wall at the end of a hidden crack.

They have opened it.
