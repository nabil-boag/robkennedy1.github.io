The goblins **Yuck Yuck** and **Spider Bait** guide travellers across the **Silken Paths** for two gold a head per day. The party had no money whatsoever, so **Thibaud** promised them **a favour instead** — unspecified, to be claimed whenever the goblins choose.

They were delighted with the arrangement. *Favours are a powerful currency.*
