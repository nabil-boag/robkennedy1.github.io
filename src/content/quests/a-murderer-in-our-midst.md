Discover who strangled **Eldeth Feldrun** in her sleep and left her body posed as a sacrifice.

**Solved.** For weeks suspicion rested on the unstable drow **Sarith**. The answer came from a **nothic** in Dhurgrimm, whose eye reads secrets: the killer was **Buppido**, the friendly derro who had walked beside the party all the way from Velkynvelve. He admitted it — a sacrifice to his god, to win their escape — and chose Eldeth because she was a surface dwarf. The party executed him.
