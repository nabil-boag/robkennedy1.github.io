Help **Shuushar the Awakened** carry his teachings of peace and meditation back to his people, so that they too might avoid the madness that plagues the kuo-toa.

His home is **Sloobludop**, on the shore of the **Darklake** — roughly a week's travel west, and he will not promise the journey is a safe one. With the west passage taken, this is the direction the party is now heading.
