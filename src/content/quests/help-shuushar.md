Help **Shuushar the Awakened** carry his teachings of peace and meditation back to his people, so that they too might avoid the madness that plagues the kuo-toa.

The party got him home. It made no difference: his own people called him a heretic, seized the party as sacrifices, and were tearing each other apart over two gods by the time **Demogorgon** rose out of the lake and flattened the town — with Shuushar in it.

He never got to teach them, and there is hardly anyone left to teach.
