Something in the Underdark is getting into people's heads, and the party is no longer merely hearing about it.

**Sarith** has complained of splitting pain since Velkynvelve. **Shuushar** said his whole people are prone to madness, and that it had been getting worse — his life of peace and meditation was an attempt to escape it. The Sea Mother's own archpriest said the same of his congregation.

Then the party walked a narrow passage whose walls were riddled with holes, out of which came moaning, shrieking and gibbering in no language any of them knew. **Kit** was left paralysed for four minutes and **Ilyrion** unconscious for six, and both came out of it carrying something they hadn't gone in with. The **spectator** on the Silken Paths spoke into their minds with thoughts *tinged with madness* — and warned them that demons are rising.

Looking at **Demogorgon** was worse still — **Rhys** had to be carried out of Sloobludop babbling.

And then it started giving things back. On the road east, three of them found they could do things they could not do before, and all three date it to that tunnel. **Rhys** stepped out of himself and fought beside his own double. **Kit** watched a blade of blue-green light form in his hand, and later discovered he could speak into **Thibaud's** mind from across a lake shore. **Ilyrion** has pointed out, uneasily, that his own conjured sword never worked until he came down here either.

Nobody is calling it a blessing.
