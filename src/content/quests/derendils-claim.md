title: "The Prince of Nelrindenvane"
kind: lore

order: 9
---

The quaggoth **Derendil** claims to be an elven prince of **Nelrindenvane**, a kingdom in the High Forest. He says the evil wizard **Terrestor** transformed him into a quaggoth and seized his realm. Whether the tale is true, no one yet knows.
