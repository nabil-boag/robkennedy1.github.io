On the road to Sloobludop the party watched four **hook horrors** shamble past their camp in the night — hunched, hook-armed, crusted with **orange spores**, and moving like something else was doing the walking.

Weeks later **Sarith** stopped clutching his head. The pain that had tormented him since Velkynvelve simply went, and in its place came a cheerful eagerness to get to a big city full of people. Looking closely, the party found the same **orange spores growing in his skin**.

Adult myconids met in a side cavern answered the question through the mycelium — the network that connects every growing thing in the Underdark, through which they say *hurt one, you hurt them all*. Their answer was two words:

> **Corruption is spreading.**

They could not say what it was, or what to do about it. Nobody has told Sarith. Somebody suggested a mercy killing, and was talked out of it.
