---
name: "The Lost Tomb of Kaine"
status: mentioned
order: 10
---

A tomb somewhere in the Underdark, said by the halfling **Fergus Rumblefoot** to hold treasure. He was hunting it with three companions when gnolls killed them and spiders took him.

He says he can still find the way. Asked what guards it, he answered: spiders. Neither Shuushar nor anyone else the party asked had ever heard of Kaine.
