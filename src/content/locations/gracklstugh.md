A **duergar** city on the far side of the Darklake, and **Buppido's** home — he means to travel with the party a while yet, then make his own way back to it.

By his account it is enormous and dangerous and he is very fond of it. It is less known for fair maidens than for its **choking smog**, thrown up by the many furnaces that burn there, and it is home to a **red dragon** that keeps those furnaces lit. Thibaud offered to slay it. Buppido pointed out that this would be killing the local industry.
