The home of the myconid **Stool** and his people. Stool has asked the party to escort him there, promising they will be given sanctuary among his kind.

Adult myconids met on the road east finally pinned down where it is: **north of Gracklstugh**. Stool, told the party were going to the duergar city first, was entirely content — *happy, we're friends* — and is coming along.
