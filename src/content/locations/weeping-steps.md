A long stretch of the Darklake's eastern shore where the cliff falls away in terraces — hundreds of shallow pools brimming one into the next, lit blue by glowing mushrooms, loud with the sound of water going down.

**Buppido** knew of it by reputation and recommended it as a good place to find food. It was better than that. After seven weeks of tunnels, dead air and total darkness, it was the first place the party saw that was simply beautiful, and they spent a night bathing in it, eating crab and flatbread ground from mushroom spores, and sleeping clean.

It is not entirely safe — three goblins came over one of the steps at them — and the falling water is loud enough to swallow anything approaching. It was also where **Ilyrion** first reached out and found his patron waiting.
