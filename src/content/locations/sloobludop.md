A kuo-toa settlement on the shore of the **Darklake**, and the home of **Shuushar the Awakened**. Reaching it means travelling **west** from Velkynvelve — about a week, by Shuushar's reckoning, and he will not promise the party a safe one.

It is also where the party owes a debt: they promised the duergar trader **Grimnar** a good word at Sloobludop in exchange for misdirecting the drow pursuit.
