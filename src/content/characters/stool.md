A myconid the party saved from being bullied by **Ront**. As a result Stool is extremely friendly to them — especially **Rhys** and **Thibaud**, who stood up for him. He can release spores that let creatures within thirty feet **communicate telepathically**, letting the prisoners talk past any language barrier and plan their escape. Stool longs to return home to his people in **Neverlight Grove**, and has asked the party to come with him, promising they will be given sanctuary.
