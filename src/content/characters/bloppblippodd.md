Daughter of **Ploopploopeen**, and archpriest of the **Deep Father** — the new god whose cult had taken hold in **Sloobludop** in the two years since Shuushar left it.

Her people took the party prisoner on the shore road as **offerings**. It was her ceremony the party were paraded into: an idol of stretched hide, dead manta ray and pinned octopuses, and a blood-stained pit twenty feet in front of it.

Her father described her as earthy, strong and cruel, and said the last thing she was in was distress. He accepted her god publicly, presented the party as his offering to her, and then clubbed her to the ground and killed her on her own altar.

As she died she reached out towards the lake and screamed. Something answered.
