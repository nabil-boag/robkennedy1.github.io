A halfling the party cut out of a **spider's cocoon** halfway across the Silken Paths, having been hanging there a few hours and no longer able to walk. Thibaud carried him the rest of the way, and told him repeatedly that it was Thibaud who had saved him.

He was hunting the **Lost Tomb of Kaine** with three companions when a pack of gnolls killed them all and the spiders took him. He is entirely undeterred: he still wants the treasure, still says he can find the way, and now wants the party to come with him — his last party, as he points out, were fully clothed and it did them no good at all.
