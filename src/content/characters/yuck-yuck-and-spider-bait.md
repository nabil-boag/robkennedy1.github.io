Two goblins who make a living leading travellers across the **Silken Paths**, the vast spiderweb network strung over a chasm on the road to the Darklake. They practise every day, coat their feet in grease, and **surf** the webs at speed for the sheer pleasure of it.

Their price is two gold per person per day. The party had no money at all, so **Thibaud** bought passage with **a favour, to be named later** — a bargain the goblins seemed far more pleased with than the coin.

They were honest guides: they warned about the spiders, they let the party stop to cut a stranger out of a cocoon, and when a spectator attacked they withdrew to *supervise the situation from a safe distance*, exactly as promised. They melted away when the kuo-toa took the party prisoner outside Sloobludop.
