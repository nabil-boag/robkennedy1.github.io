Twin deep gnomes who avoided all contact with the other prisoners.

They escaped Velkynvelve with everyone else and then **slipped away in the night** on the second day of the journey west, while the watch was looking elsewhere. There were no tracks to follow by the time anyone noticed, and the party was a full day past the last junction. Nobody ever did learn anything about them.
