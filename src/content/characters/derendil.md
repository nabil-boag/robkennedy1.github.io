A quaggoth who confided to **Rhys** that he is in fact an elven prince, ousted from his kingdom of **Nelrindenvane** in the High Forest by the evil wizard **Terrestor** — who, he claims, turned him into a quaggoth and seized his realm. He is friendly to the party.

Whatever he was once, he is formidable now. On the **Silken Paths** it was Derendil who closed with the spectator that had just killed **Jimjar**, opened with a critical hit, and a round later tore the creature apart entirely.
