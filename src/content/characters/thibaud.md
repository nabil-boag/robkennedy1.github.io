A vain and wealthy human fighter of **House Comme Ça Jean** — by his own account a charitable house and "a big name on the Sword Coast" — with a weakness for fine textiles. His first instinct in captivity was to try to **bribe the drow** with his family's name and money.

He helped Rhys stand up to **Ront** on the myconid Stool's behalf. His persistent questioning of the unstable drow **Sarith** — despite being told to back off — sparked a fistfight before Jebediah intervened. Set to work the lift, he proved too weak to turn the pulley and faked a fall onto the platform, to no avail. Through Stool's spores he learned that the deep gnome **Jimjar** is in truth a surface spy.

He is in the Underdark to earn his knighthood, and is desperate for someone to write the deeds down for his father. He is accumulating some: he cut Fergus out of a spider's cocoon and insisted the halfling remember who saved him, impersonated a goddess at the Deep Father's altar, and killed two bandits with a borrowed spear before taking the captain's head off with it.

He came out of the bandit fight in **studded leather** and carrying a **pistol** — a fancy newfangled thing with powder in it that nobody in the party knows how to load. He also ate a handful of **green slime** because he was sick of mushrooms, and had to be talked through vomiting it back up before it rotted through him.
