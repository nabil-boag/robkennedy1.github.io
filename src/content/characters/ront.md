A male orc, very unfriendly to the party. He was caught bullying the myconid **Stool**, prompting the party to intervene. **Rhys** played on Ront's vanity and persuaded him to break free of his chains in a show of strength — but the guards saw it. The drow shot Ront with poison crossbow bolts, and **Ilvara** had him thrown into the spider webs surrounding the outpost, where he was devoured by giant spiders. Rhys felt deep guilt over his role in the events that led to Ront's death.
