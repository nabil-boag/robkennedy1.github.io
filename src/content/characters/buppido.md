A male derro, and a fellow prisoner of the drow. He is friendly to the party, and endlessly chatty.

On the road west he told **Thibaud** that he comes from **Gracklstugh**, a duergar city on the far side of the Darklake — huge, dangerous, thick with furnace smog, and home to a **red dragon** that keeps the forges burning. He intends to travel with the party for a while and then make his own way home. He also proved useful in the fungus swamp, pointing out the cask-shaped fungi that hold drinkable water.
