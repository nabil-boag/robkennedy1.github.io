A male derro, and a fellow prisoner of the drow. For weeks he was the friendliest of all the party's companions: endlessly chatty, always helpful.

On the road west he told **Thibaud** that he comes from **Gracklstugh**, a duergar city on the far side of the Darklake — huge, dangerous, thick with furnace smog, and home to a **red dragon** that keeps the forges burning. He pointed out the cask-shaped fungi that hold drinkable water in the swamp, and the ripplebark that turned out to be good roasted. He fought alongside the party, and was knocked out by an ochre jelly on the bridge above the torchstalk.

It was all a front. In **Dhurgrimm**, a **nothic** turned its secret-reading eye on him and laughed: he had killed someone while everyone was asleep, and let somebody else take the blame.

Tied up and questioned afterwards, he didn't deny it. **He strangled Eldeth Feldrun** in the slave pen at Velkynvelve and **sacrificed her to his god** to get them all out — and the next morning, they escaped. He chose her because she was a dwarf from the surface, and his people hate surface dwarves. He felt no guilt: *we do not feel guilt for purging the wicked.*

The party felt none either. **Joy** killed him where he lay, with a scimitar to the head, in the violet light of the faerzress.
