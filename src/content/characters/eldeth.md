A female dwarf, friendly to the party. She formed a bond with **Ilyrion** over kitchen duty, after he suggested they help each other escape, showed he could magically create weapons, and stole food to share with her and the other prisoners. She believed that if anyone could escape, it would be him — and asked that, should she not make it, Ilyrion return her **shield and warhammer** to her family in **Gauntlgrym**.

She was **murdered in her sleep** during the night — strangled, a livid red mark left around her neck, her body posed kneeling as though offered up to something. **Jebediah** recognised it as a sacrifice to the murder god **Diinkarazan**, though strangely bloodless: such offerings are normally made by cutting the victim open.
