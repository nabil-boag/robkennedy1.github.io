A male drow prisoner, unstable and seemingly not in his right mind, forever complaining of a terrible pain in his head. He was unfriendly to the party and came to blows with **Thibaud**, who agitated him by refusing to stop asking questions; **Jebediah** managed to talk him down. Sarith says he is imprisoned because he was framed for murdering another drow. After **Ilyrion** showed him kindness with stolen food, a calmer Sarith revealed that he knows there is an **ooze in the pool** at the bottom of the waterfall.

He was right. The moment the party splashed down into that pool and the grey ooze rose out of it, Sarith was there to say *I told you*. He escaped with them and travels with the party still — no better, muttering about killing, and nobody has yet unlocked his shackles.
