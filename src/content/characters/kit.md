A scruffy, scrawny human rogue — scarred and crooked-nosed from a hard-luck life. Set to work the outpost's lift alongside Thibaud. It was Kit who turned the prisoners' whispered gossip into a plan — proposing they exploit **Jorlan's** bitterness and persuade him to let them escape, so that the blame and humiliation would fall on **Shoor** and **Ilvara**.

He grew up around goblin children on the street and speaks their language, which has twice now talked the party out of a fight in the dark.

Something changed in him after the **screaming tunnel**, where he collapsed. Mid-fight with a band of highwaymen he swung his lead pipe and a **blue-green blade formed in his hand out of nothing** — he dropped it in fright and it was gone before it hit the ground. Later, walking the lake shallows well outside the range of Stool's spores, he found he could put words straight into **Thibaud's** head. Only Thibaud's. Only one way.

He has no explanation for either.
