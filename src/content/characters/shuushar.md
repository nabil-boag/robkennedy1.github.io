A male kuo-toa, friendly and serene. He revealed that he lives a life of peace and meditation to escape the madness that befalls so many of his kind. He comforted **Rhys** when the rogue was distraught over his part in Ront's death. Shuushar wished to return to his people and pass on his learnings, so that others of his kind might avoid madness too.

He guided the party west out of Velkynvelve towards his home at **Sloobludop** — seven days of tunnels, swamp and spiderweb — answering endless questions about the Underdark along the way, and warning them honestly that his people were erratic, that he was not popular there for preaching peace, and that a new religion had been stirring up trouble when he left.

He was right on every count. His own people took the party prisoner on the shore road and called him a **heretic** to his face.

He was watching sadly from the crowd when the party sprang **Ploopploopeen's** trap, and he said sorry into their minds for what his home had become. Moments later **Demogorgon** rose out of the Darklake, and its second tentacle came down on the place where he was standing.

He never got to teach them.
