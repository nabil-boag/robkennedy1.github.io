A male kuo-toa, friendly and serene. He revealed that he lives a life of peace and meditation to escape the madness that befalls so many of his kind. He comforted **Rhys** when the rogue was distraught over his part in Ront's death. Shuushar wishes to return to his people and pass on his learnings, so that others of his kind might avoid madness too.
