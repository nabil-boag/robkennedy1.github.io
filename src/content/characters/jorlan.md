A maimed drow warrior who lost his looks and his fighting skill in a fight with a **black pudding** (a corrosive Underdark ooze), and who bitterly resents **Shoor** — the rival who replaced him at **Ilvara's** side. The party approached him through Stool's telepathic spores and struck a bargain: he would let them escape, and in return they would make sure the blame fell on Shoor. Jorlan agreed — though he warned that if they betrayed him he would hunt them down and torture them for days — promising to leave the slave-pen gate open and create a distraction at the next changing of the guard. He advised the party to head first to the **armoury** in the guard tower, then jump into the **spider webs** below, make their way to the **pool**, and escape from there.
