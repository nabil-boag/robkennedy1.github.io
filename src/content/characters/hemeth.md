The other prisoner on the altar at Sloobludop — the one who saw the party looking shifty and said *if you're up to something, get me out of here*. They cut him loose in the chaos and he came out with them.

He gives his name as Hemeth, says he is an **arms dealer from Gracklstugh**, and that he was trading at Sloobludop when the kuo-toa took him from behind and put him up for sacrifice. He wants to go home, and has offered the party a good price on weapons when they get there.

Two of the party leaned on him about his story. He is telling the truth about the arms dealing — and he is **holding something back**. He and **Buppido** talked to each other constantly in Undercommon, which nobody else in the party speaks.

He says Sloobludop was not even the first time someone tried to sacrifice him: the drow once tried to give him to their spider goddess.

He is far more dangerous than he looks. Fighting the nothics and grells in Dhurgrimm, he grew to giant size — a duergar talent — and put his war pick straight through a grell's brain, then hacked the last nothic to pieces. It was also Hemeth who recognised the faerzress, and warned that lately people have been bursting into flames in it.
