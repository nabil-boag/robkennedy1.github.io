The other prisoner on the altar at Sloobludop — the one who saw the party looking shifty and said *if you're up to something, get me out of here*. They cut him loose in the chaos and he came out with them.

He gives his name as Hemeth, says he is an **arms dealer from Gracklstugh**, and that he was trading at Sloobludop when the kuo-toa took him from behind and put him up for sacrifice. He wants to go home, and has offered the party a good price on weapons when they get there.

Two of the party leaned on him about his story. He is telling the truth about the arms dealing — and he is **holding something back**. He and **Buppido** talk to each other constantly in Undercommon, which nobody else in the party speaks.
