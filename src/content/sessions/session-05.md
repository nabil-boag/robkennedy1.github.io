Half the party sits this one out. **Ilyrion**, **Jebediah** and **Joy** carry on east with Buppido, Hemeth, Fergus, Sarith and Stool — and by the end of it, one of those companions will be dead at their hands.

## The jelly on the bridge

The session opens where the last one stopped: halfway across a rope bridge above a valley of explosive torchstalk, with something yellow sliding along the planks towards them.

It is an **ochre jelly**. It reaches **Buppido** first and lays him out with one slap of burning acid. Everywhere it moves it leaves a trail that eats into the rope and wood holding the bridge up. Jebediah's guiding bolt bursts it into two smaller jellies; Ilyrion's *arms of Hadar* and a blast of hellish rebuke finish them off. Joy lays hands on Buppido and brings him round.

They get off that bridge as fast as they can.

## Sarith talks

Fergus says the **Lost Tomb of Khaem** lies two days beyond a huge abandoned city built into the cliffs along the lake. On the way, the party decide to try the **tongue of madness** they picked up — a mushroom that makes you speak every thought aloud for an hour — and slip it into **Sarith's** breakfast.

What comes out is mostly the same thing on repeat. He wants to go to the big city. He wants to be surrounded by people. His head hurts so much and it never stops, but it will be so good to be among lots of people. He hasn't always liked people — only recently. He doesn't see any orange spots on his skin. He didn't kill anyone.

It is not reassuring.

On watch that night, Ilyrion reaches for his patron again. It feels slightly closer than before.

## Dhurgrimm, the Rotted Warren

The city comes out of the dark late the next day: a cliff covered from shore to roof in **enormous shelf-shaped fungi**, each one hollowed out with doors and windows, joined by rope bridges and stairways climbing up into the dark. It was built by **deep gnomes** a very long time ago. Nobody knows what happened to them.

It is completely empty. There is no furniture, no food, no water — nothing at all, not even ordinary mushrooms. Detect magic finds nothing. They spend two days walking through it, from one hollow shelf to the next, and at one point find a whole shelf collapsed into a gorge that takes a full day to climb down into and back out of.

By the second day they have run out of water, they are high above the lake with no way down to it, and they have no idea how far the city goes on. They start to jog.

## The violet light

Then a **purple glow** appears ahead, lighting up the fungus around it. **Hemeth** recognises it: **faerzress**, the strange magic that soaks parts of the Underdark. It always used to be fairly harmless, he says — but lately he has heard of people bursting into flames in it. Ilyrion identifies it as something close to **wild magic**, and the party agree not to cast spells inside it.

They have to go through it anyway. They send Sarith in first. He is fine. His head hurts.

## What the nothic saw

Inside the glow, a creature is hunched over its meal: grey, scaly, spined, with clawed hands, big teeth and **a single huge eye** that takes up most of its head. It is perfectly polite. The meat is definitely not a person, it says.

Its eye glows, briefly, at Ilyrion.

> You're communing with some monster down in the Underdark, and not telling your friends here. How deceptive of you.

So it comes out. **Ilyrion** admits to the others that his power comes from something out there in the dark — something that feels like Demogorgon, but isn't.

The creature — a **nothic** — offers more insights. The party, curious, point it at **Buppido**. Its eye glows, and it starts to laugh:

> Oh my, this is much better than I was expecting. You killed her while they were all asleep — and they blamed somebody else, and had no idea.

Buppido says he doesn't know what it's talking about.

Then the nothic calls to its friend, and something floats down out of the darkness overhead: a **grell**, a hovering brain with a hooked beak and a skirt of paralysing tentacles. A second nothic and a second grell come out behind the party. The first nothic calls them **Itsy, Bitsy, Teeny and Weeny**.

## Itsy, Bitsy, Teeny and Weeny

It is the hardest fight they have had. The nothics' **rotting gaze** burns people from the inside out from across the cavern; the grells grab, peck and paralyse. **Ilyrion** goes down, grappled in tentacles, and comes back on a natural 20 death save. **Buppido** goes down. **Jebediah** goes down. A misty step in the faerzress nearly goes wrong.

**Hemeth**, it turns out, can make himself huge — a duergar trick — and once enlarged he puts his war pick straight through one grell's brain and later hacks the last nothic to pieces. **Joy** cuts the other grell apart with a blazing smite. **Jebediah's** guiding bolts do the rest.

## Buppido

When it is over, they tie Buppido up with strips cut from a spare suit of leather armour and bring him round.

He doesn't deny it. He strangled **Eldeth** in the slave pen and **sacrificed her to his god**, he says, to get them all out of Velkynvelve — and the next morning, they escaped. He chose her because she was a dwarf from the surface, and his kind hate surface dwarves. Jebediah, a surface dwarf, is standing right there.

He feels no guilt. *We do not feel guilt for purging the wicked.*

The party don't either. **Joy** brings her scimitar down on his head, and Buppido — chatty, friendly, endlessly helpful Buppido — bleeds out on the floor of the violet light.

Ilyrion, who promised Eldeth he would carry her shield and hammer home and has failed to do it, takes some comfort in that.

## Old friends and a voice in the dark

At the far end of the city the steps lead down to a **forest of mushrooms** of every shape and size, many of them glowing. **Stool** runs into it shrieking with delight. They rest at its edge.

In the morning, a familiar voice: **Grimnar** the duergar trader and his armoured pack beasts, who greets them as his "sex friends" and says he almost didn't recognise them with their clothes on. He kept his side of the bargain — he sent the drow off in completely the wrong direction. The party have to tell him there is no Sloobludop left to put in a good word at.

Grimnar has heard of the tomb, though — or at least of **Khaem**: a very powerful sorceress from long ago, who lived somewhere floating and flew about the place.

They sell him the bandit captain's **pistol** for 120 gold (all he is carrying) and spend it: tools for Jebediah to make things on the road, two **healing potions**, rations, and two **holy symbols** — one for Rhys, and one, at long last, for Jebediah.

The next day, deep in the mushroom forest, everyone hears the same thing at once: a soft woman's voice in their heads, faint and far away.

> Hello? Is someone there? Please, I need your help. I have been trapped in the dark for so long. So very long. Won't you help to free me?

Fergus has heard of this. People who went looking for the tomb spoke of a voice.

They are suddenly sure which way the entrance lies, without knowing how they know. It leads them to a narrow crack in a mushroom-covered wall, down a long sideways shuffle, and out in front of a stained marble wall with a deep-set door of stone sheathed in green, ancient bronze. Ilyrion's detect magic finds faerzress on the other side.

They put their shoulders to it. The door opens.
