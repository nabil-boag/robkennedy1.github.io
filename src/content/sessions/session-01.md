The party awakens stripped of equipment and dignity — barefoot, in nothing but their underwear, **manacled and collared**, the two bound together by a heavy chain. They have been captured by the drow and thrown into a slave pen in the Underdark outpost of **Velkynvelve**, alongside ten other prisoners. Their captor is **Mistress Ilvara of House Mizzrym**, a cruel drow priestess who rules with a scourge in hand, fond of reminding the prisoners that their lives now belong to her.

## A grim arrival

A slave dwarf named **Eldeth Feldrun** explains the situation: the prisoners are being held until a drow party from the city of **Menzoberranzan** arrives to march them off to be sold as slaves. That party is late — expected days ago and still nowhere to be seen. The vain and wealthy **Thibaud**, of **House Comme Ça Jean**, floats buying their freedom with his family's name, but no one truly expects the drow to free slaves on a promise.

The captives speak to **Sarith**, a drow prisoner who proves hostile and erratic, forever complaining of a splitting pain in his head. When **Thibaud** ignores Sarith's warnings to leave him be, Sarith snaps and the two come to blows. **Jebediah** manages to calm him with soothing words before things turn deadly.

Then the party witnesses the orc bully **Ront** tormenting a small humanoid mushroom — a myconid named **Stool**. **Thibaud** and **Rhys** intervene. Rather than fight, Rhys flatters Ront's vanity and goads him into a show of strength: the orc shatters his own chains. The guards see it. They drop Ront with poison crossbow bolts, and when the outpost's leaders arrive, the commander **Ilvara** makes a public example of him — ordering **Shoor** to hurl the unconscious orc into the giant spiders' webs at the cavern's edge, where Ront is devoured. Rhys is left wracked with guilt over his part in the orc's death.

## A day of chores

The prisoners are split up and sent to forced labour:

- **Rhys and Jebediah** collect chamber pots from the slave pens and drow barracks. They see soldiers resting on pallets, armour and weapons stowed beneath, belongings locked in chests beside them. Rhys quietly picks a chest without waking its owner and steals an **iron bar** and a **vial of drow poison**. Here he also hears the quaggoth **Derendil** insist that he is truly an elven prince, transformed into a beast by an evil wizard who then stole his kingdom.
- **Thibaud and Kit** are set to work the lift between the cavern floor and the outpost. Thibaud is too weak to turn the pulley; two quaggoths take over. He fakes a fall onto the lift among the drow, but they simply order him hauled back up.
- **Ilyrion** draws kitchen duty in the main hall. He confides in **Eldeth** that he can conjure weapons once outside the pen — though some ward inside the pen smothers all magic. He proposes they escape together and steal food to share with the others. Impressed, Eldeth comes to believe Ilyrion could truly get out, and asks that, should she not survive, he return her **shield and warhammer** to her family in **Gauntlgrym**.

## Whispers in the dark

Back in the pen that night, **Stool** thanks his rescuers by releasing spores that let everyone within thirty feet **communicate telepathically**, bypassing every language barrier. He reveals he is a myconid longing to return home to **Neverlight Grove**, and offers to lead the party there under his people's protection.

The prisoners trade secrets through the spores:

- **Jimjar**, a deep gnome, admits to **Thibaud** that he is a spy from the surface, sent to investigate the fate of a former deep gnome city in the Underdark. He also reveals the rift among the drow leaders: Ilvara once kept the warrior **Jorlan** as her lover and second-in-command, but cast him aside — for both roles — in favour of **Shoor** after a **black pudding** — a corrosive Underdark ooze — disfigured him.
- **Shuushar**, a serene kuo-toa, tells **Rhys** that he has devoted himself to peace and meditation to escape the madness that claims so many of his kind, and wishes to carry that wisdom back to his people.
- Seizing on Jimjar's gossip, **Kit** proposes a plan: exploit Jorlan's bitterness, and persuade him to let the prisoners escape so the blame — and the humiliation — falls on Shoor and Ilvara.

## Dark dreams and a murder

As the party sleeps, **Jebediah, Rhys, and Ilyrion** are visited by terrible dreams — groping shadows in endless tunnels, oily tentacles brushing their skin, a rising buzz and howl, the wounds of madmen bursting with maggots and flies. Rhys and Ilyrion wake with a start at the very same instant. All three are left certain that something **far worse than the drow** waits in the depths.

Morning brings tragedy: **Eldeth has been murdered in her sleep** — strangled, a livid red mark around her neck, her body arranged kneeling and posed as though offered up to something. Drawing on his religious training, **Jebediah** recognises it as an offering to **Diinkarazan**, an Underdark god of murder — though strangely bloodless, for such sacrifices are normally made by cutting the victim open. The party is left uneasy, with no idea who among them is the killer; suspicion falls on the unstable **Sarith**, but nothing is certain. An indifferent guard simply calls for help removing the corpse.

Soon after, **Jorlan** himself appears. Through Stool's spores the party makes their offer: help us escape, and the blame will fall on Shoor. Jorlan agrees — though he warns that if they betray him, he will hunt them down and torture them for days. At the next changing of the guard — thirty minutes away — he will **leave the gate unlocked and create a distraction**. His advice: make for the **armoury in the guard tower** above the pen, then leap into the **spider webs** below, work down to the **pool**, and escape from there.

The session ends on the cusp of the break-out, the clock already running.
